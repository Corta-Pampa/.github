Corta designs, deploys and maintains private artificial intelligence infrastructure tailored to business needs.

Our approach: AI deployed in your environment, configured for your workflows and integrated with your existing tools.

## What we do

We operate across the entire AI stack, from infrastructure to everyday business applications.

- **Private AI infrastructure** — Deploying AI models on local servers.
- **Agentic systems** — Agents that can search, analyze, organize and produce documents.
- **Business automation** — Automating workflows and repetitive tasks.
- **Data integration** — Secure connections to files, databases and existing tools.
- **Custom capabilities** — Developing skills and automations tailored to specific business activities.
- **Maintenance & support** — Monitoring, updates, backups, security and ongoing support.

## Our approach

We believe AI should adapt to businesses, not the other way around.

Our deployments are built around a few fundamental principles:

- **Data sovereignty** — Data stays within the client's environment.
- **Privacy by design** — Prioritizing local models and minimizing external dependencies.
- **Open source first** — Building on open, sustainable technologies whenever possible.
- **Human oversight** — Keeping people in control of decisions and sensitive operations.
- **Security first** — Isolated environments, permission management and controlled access.
- **Built for the long run** — Reproducible, documented and maintainable deployments.

## Our focus

### AI for property & land professionals

Our first area of focus is property valuation and land expertise.

We build AI environments that help professionals work with their case files, archives and documentary resources.

Key applications include:

- Analysis of valuation reports and legal documents.
- Cross-document search across internal archives.
- Research on easements, commercial leases and land parcels.
- Information extraction and structuring.
- Generation of professional reports and documents.
- Automation of administrative and business workflows.

Our goal is not to replace human expertise, but to give professionals more time to focus on what creates real value.

## Technology

We build our solutions around open and modular technologies.

| Layer | Technologies |
|---|---|
| Agent framework | Hermes Agent |
| Language models | Qwen and other open-weight LLMs |
| Inference | Ollama, vLLM |
| Infrastructure | Linux, Ubuntu Server |
| Deployment | Docker, Docker Compose |
| Integration | MCP, APIs, filesystem |
| Monitoring | System health, logs and backups |

Components are selected and configured according to the technical and operational requirements of each deployment.
